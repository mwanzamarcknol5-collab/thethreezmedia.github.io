LABEL HISTORY & BACKGROUND

The Journey of The Threez Media

Marcknol Mwanza — CEO & Founder
Original Founders (2017): CEO Marcknol Mwanza, John Mbembe, & Bramuel Juma

The story of The Threez Media began back in 2017 during our days at St Romano's Matawa Secondary School. What started as simple classroom banter, comedy writing, and creative scripts between three close friends eventually laid the foundation for an independent creative label.

"From writing skits during high school breaks at St Romano's Matawa to producing full digital media productions in Nairobi, our passion for storytelling has remained unchanged."

Founded by Marcknol Mwanza alongside co-founders Bramuel Juma and John Mbembe, the group set out to create comedic skits, spoken word performances, and social satire tailored for digital audiences across Kenya.

Today, based out of Nairobi, The Threez Media continues to expand its reach—producing digital content, social media satire, and creative scripts while remaining true to the roots where it all started.
